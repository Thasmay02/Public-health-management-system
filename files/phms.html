About PHMS

The Public Health Management System (PHMS) is a comprehensive, centralized platform built to modernize healthcare administration. Designed for hospitals, clinics, and government health departments, PHMS bridges the gap between healthcare providers and patients. Our mission is to streamline clinical workflows, ensure real-time disease surveillance, simplify inventory management, and empower patients with direct access to their medical histories and healthcare professionals.
